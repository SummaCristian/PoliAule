The first piece of a much bigger redesign of PoliAule.

## Tab bar
- An iOS-like design, with smoother animations and more responsiveness
- On mobile it sits at the bottom of the screen, easy to reach with your thumb
- On desktop it sits in the top left corner
- Refined animations make it feel more alive
- Tap or click to switch tab, or **drag**: grab the selection pill and drag it to the tab you want
