A brand new icon and logo for PoliAule, shown everywhere: your bookmarks and your installed PWA, on Android or iOS, all get the shiny new logo.

The last component of the Available tab still waiting for its redesign is finally done too: the Campus picker now ties in with the rest of the UI.

## New logo
PoliAule now has a proper logo!

![PoliAule's logo](media/logo.svg)

It's used in various places throughout the UI, in the website's favicon, and in the PWA's app icon on both iOS and Android.

## Campus picker
The Campus picker leaves the standard browser picker behind for a custom, chip-based design.

The new layout separates Milano's campuses from the others and groups them by location. Tapping "Città Studi" lets you pick "Leonardo" or "Colombo", while "Bovisa" shows both "Durando" and "La Masa".
