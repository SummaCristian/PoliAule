A whole lot of UX improvements. You can finally customise PoliAule's behaviour in the way that best suits your student needs.

## Localisation
PoliAule now supports both English and **Italian**. It picks your preferred language from your browser's settings, and you can change it at any time in Settings.

## Time indicators
Hover on the timeline of an available classroom to see the time you're pointing at. This makes the graph easier to read and shows exactly when the classroom will or won't be free.

## Settings
There's a gear icon in the top right corner: tap it to find PoliAule's all-new Settings, where you can tweak almost anything:

- Language
- 12h/24h time format
- Hide Sundays (off by default)
- Default time interval: the pickers default to a 1-hour query, but you can make it up to 12 hours
- Auto collapse: show buildings collapsed in the results (off by default)
- Show partially free: show or hide classrooms that aren't free for the whole queried period (on by default)
- Preferred campus: the campus selected when the page loads
- Remember last used campus: open on the campus you picked last time

> [!NOTE]
> The last two settings are mutually exclusive.
