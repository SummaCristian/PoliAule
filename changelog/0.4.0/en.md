## Time pickers
The new Time pickers are designed for quick interactions. On mobile they show a preview of the selected time and open the full UI in a modal on tap. On desktop the full UI is shown when there's enough room for it.

The full picker can move the selection forward and backward in 1-hour jumps, and suggests relevant choices like "now", "1 hour from now", or "1 hour after the start time" for the "To" picker.
