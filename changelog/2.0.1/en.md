Just a few days after 2.0, this release is about sanding off the rough edges I found after launch.

The headline is the animations: the pickers now open and close more smoothly, and the lens of the Date picker bends the light like the rest of the glass in PoliAule.

![The pickers opening and closing](media/pickers_v201.mp4#loop "The refined picker animations")

## Other changes
- If you've already allowed location access, the Campus map now adds your position on its own
- The Campus sheet's position and material were adjusted so it sits properly with the tab bar and the content is more legible
- The "Generally closed" box in the classroom page has a tinted shadow, matching the rest of the page
- Fixed a misalignment of graduation days in the Date picker, and a bug that appeared in it after an update
- Fixed a bug with changelogs when switching language
