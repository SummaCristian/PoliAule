## Search
Search now opens in an overlay on top of whichever tab you were on. On mobile the search field sits at the bottom, right above the keyboard; on desktop it sits at the top centre of the screen.

## Lessons and exams
PoliAule now builds an index of every occupation it finds, so you can search for lessons and exams in the new Search screen.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- `1` / `2` to switch tab
- `S` or `/` to open Search
- `Esc` to close dialogs and Search
- `Ctrl` / `Cmd` + `,` to open Settings
- `?` to show the list of shortcuts
- Basic arrow-key navigation in some places

## Building view
In the available classrooms results you can now jump from one building to another. Tap the building name pill in the top left of the section (it's sticky) to see all the buildings in that campus, with how many classrooms are free and how many aren't in each, then tap a building to jump back to the list, right at that building.

## Pickers
The pickers now have their own section, placed according to the layout. On desktop they live in the left column, always expanded, in a mosaic-like layout. On mobile they sit in their compact form in a sticky bar above the results; tap one and it morphs into its expanded version to change the selection.
